OSRS High Alch Guide

Everything you need to profit from High Level Alchemy

What is High Level Alchemy?

High Level Alchemy (commonly called High Alch) is a Magic spell available at level 55. It converts any item directly into gold coins, returning 60% of the item's base store value. Combined with a Nature rune and five Fire runes per cast, it's one of the most accessible money-making methods in Old School RuneScape — and one of the few that can generate profit while simultaneously training Magic.

The spell has a 5-tick cast time, meaning you can cast it roughly 1,200 times per hour when focused. At 65 Magic XP per cast, that works out to around 78,000 XP per hour — decent rates for a method that costs nothing and may even earn you gold.

Requirements

  • Level 55 Magic to cast High Alchemy
  • 1 Nature rune per cast
  • 5 Fire runes per cast (or a fire staff to avoid this cost entirely)
  • An item whose alch value exceeds its Grand Exchange buy price plus rune costs

The single best upgrade: Equip a Staff of Fire (costs around 1,500 gp). It provides unlimited fire runes, saving you 5 × fire rune price per cast — currently around 25gp per alch. Over a full hour of 1,200 casts that's 30,000gp saved in rune costs alone.

How to find profitable items

The core formula for profit per alch is:

Profit = High Alch value − GE buy price − Nature rune cost (− Fire rune cost if not using a staff)

The challenge is that Grand Exchange prices change constantly, so an item that was profitable yesterday may not be today. Our High Alch Calculator fetches live GE prices every 15 minutes and shows you the current profit for every alchable item in the game, sorted by whichever metric matters most to you.

When browsing the calculator, pay attention to three columns beyond raw profit:

  • Max profit — how much you can realistically make before hitting the GE buy limit (resets every 4 hours). A high profit-per-alch item with a buy limit of 4 is much less useful than a lower-margin item you can buy 5,000 of.
  • Profit/hr — the actual expected earnings rate, factoring in both your cast speed and the buy limit ceiling.
  • Buy limit — how many you can purchase in a 4-hour window. Items with limits of 1,000+ let you alch for extended sessions without running out of stock.

Alching while doing other activities

One of the best qualities of High Alch is that it fits into almost any activity with brief idle moments. The most popular combinations:

  • Agility courses — cast High Alch between obstacles on rooftop courses. You'll earn full Agility XP, Marks of Grace, Magic XP, and profit simultaneously. Seers' Village and Ardougne courses work especially well due to longer gaps between obstacles.
  • Waiting for respawns — if you're on a Slayer task or grinding a boss, alch between kills rather than waiting idle.
  • Waiting for GE offers — standing at the Grand Exchange is ideal alching time.
  • Banking — many players cast alch on every walk between the bank and wherever they're training.

Buy limit strategy for long sessions

If you want to alch for more than a 4-hour window without running out of items, buy multiple item types before you start. Each item has its own independent buy limit — so buying 2,000 battlestaves and 1,000 dragonhide bodies gives you enough stock for a much longer session than buying 2,000 of a single item.

Our calculator's profit/hr column already accounts for the buy limit ceiling, so you can identify which items genuinely support longer sessions versus which ones look good but run dry after 30 minutes.

Low Alchemy

Low Level Alchemy (level 21) returns only 40% of an item's store value versus High Alch's 60%, and still costs one Nature rune — just three Fire runes instead of five. It's almost never profitable for money-making, but it's useful for:

  • Players below level 55 who want Magic XP
  • Converting PvM drops that aren't worth banking but aren't worth High Alching either
  • Checking alch values — the Low Alch value is exactly 2/3 of the High Alch value

You can toggle between High and Low Alch on our calculator to compare profitability for both.

Other profitable Magic spells

Once you're comfortable with High Alch, these related methods are worth exploring:

  • Superheat Item (level 43) — smelt ores into bars without a furnace. Often profitable on steel, mithril, and adamantite bars.
  • Enchanting bolts (various levels) — enchant gem-tipped bolts for profit and faster XP than alching.
  • String Jewellery (Lunar spells, level 80) — string unstrung amulets in bulk. Very fast XP and often profitable.

Prices change daily. Always check the live calculator before buying items in bulk — an item that was 200gp profit per alch last week might be at a loss today if the GE price has risen. Refresh the calculator at the start of each session.
