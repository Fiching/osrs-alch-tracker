Superheat Item (level 43 Magic) smelts one ore into a bar without a furnace. Each cast uses 1 Nature rune + 4 Fire runes and grants 53 Magic XP plus the Smithing XP for the bar. At ~1,100 casts/hr it's a popular AFK money-making method combined with Magic training. Coal is required for steel (2), mithril (4), adamantite (6), and runite (8) bars — its live GE price is factored into all calculations.
